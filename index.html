About us

Who we are

AWM, short for Always Watching Markets, is a market research and newsletter company. We follow the Saudi market alongside global equities, interest rates, currencies and commodities every trading day and turn what happened into short, readable reports.

Our readers are investors, professionals and curious people who want to understand the markets without spending their whole day in front of charts. We do the watching, and we explain what matters and why.

Our purpose

Why AWM exists

Markets never sleep, and the volume of news, data and opinion is overwhelming. Our purpose is to cut through that noise and give people a calm, honest view of the market so they can make their own informed decisions.

Watch

We monitor the major markets across every trading session, from the Asian open through Riyadh and London to the New York close.

Understand

We look past the headlines to the data behind a move: earnings, central bank decisions, economic releases and flows.

Explain

We write it up in plain language, with the context you need and nothing you don't.
